Somnaut – Privacy Policy

Effective 10 October 2026

Summary

Somnaut is a dream journal, lucid-dreaming trainer and sleep listener. Everything you create or record stays on your iPhone. Somnaut has no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. We do not collect, sell or share personal data.

Dream journal

Dream entries, tags, moods and interpretations are stored in the app's private container on your device and in your encrypted device backups. They are never uploaded.

Microphone and speech

When you start a sleep session or use dictation, Somnaut records audio with the microphone. During sleep sessions it keeps only short clips around sounds above the room's noise floor. Speech is transcribed on-device using Apple's speech recognition; depending on your device and language, Apple may process speech on its servers under Apple's privacy policy. Clips and transcripts stay on the device and are deleted when you delete the session.

Apple Health

With your permission, Somnaut reads sleep analysis, heart rate, heart rate variability, respiratory rate and resting heart rate from Apple Health to show how your nights relate to your dreams. Health data is read on demand, displayed in the app and never stored outside Apple Health, transmitted, or used for advertising or marketing. If you enable it, Somnaut writes its own sleep sessions to Health as time in bed. You can revoke access at any time in the Health app under Sharing > Apps.

Dream interpretation

Interpretations are generated by Apple's on-device language model when available, or by a built-in symbol guide. The dream text is processed on your device and is not sent to Somnaut or any third party.

Sign in with Apple

You may create an account with Sign in with Apple. Somnaut stores only the anonymous user identifier Apple provides and, if you share it, your name and relay email, on your device. You can use Somnaut without an account. You can delete the account in Settings, which removes this information from the device; you can also stop sharing with Somnaut under Settings > Apple Account > Sign in with Apple.

Notifications

Reality-check reminders, Wake-Back-To-Bed alarms and morning reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

Children

Somnaut is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Because all data lives on your device, you control it directly: export it from Settings, delete individual entries, or delete the app to remove everything. For questions contact steven.spinosi@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will ship with an app update and the effective date above will change.
